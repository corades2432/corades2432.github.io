CORADES

Beauty Center Management Software for Windows

CORADES is a desktop application that helps beauty centers manage appointments, customer records, payments, service packages, sessions, cash records, reports and staff permissions in one place. All business data is stored locally on the user's own computer.

Optional Google integrations

  • Google Calendar: CORADES creates its own appointment calendar in the user's Google account and keeps it in sync with appointments entered in the application. It only accesses calendars it created itself.
  • Google Drive: CORADES stores encrypted backups of its own database in the user's Google Drive and can restore them. It only accesses files it created itself.

These integrations are optional. The application works without a Google account.

Türkçe

CORADES, güzellik merkezleri için randevu, müşteri, ödeme, paket, seans, kasa, rapor ve personel yönetimi sağlayan bir Windows masaüstü programıdır. İşletme verileri kullanıcının kendi bilgisayarında saklanır. Google Takvim senkronizasyonu ve Google Drive yedeklemesi isteğe bağlıdır.
